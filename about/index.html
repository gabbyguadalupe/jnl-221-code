About Me

I’m Gabby Guadalupe, a Broadcasting and Digital Journalism student at Syracuse University’s S.I. Newhouse School of Public Communications.

At Newhouse, I have built experience reporting, writing, producing and editing stories for broadcast and digital audiences. I am a part of CitrusTV's entertainment show Unpeeled, as well as an anchor on the radio show Z89.

My goal is to build a career in entertainment, digital media and journalism in New York City.
